Our Story

From a workshop in Kwun Tong to Hong Kong's custom PC engineering studio.

Who We Are

Engineers who build

FORGE Custom PC Ltd. was founded in 2016 by three computer-science graduates tired of overpriced, poorly routed prebuilt desktops. Today our 28 technicians have hand-assembled over 12,000 systems for players, studios, and engineering teams.


Every build should be transparent before a single screw is turned — which is why we built our real-time 3D configurator with physical slot and wattage verification.

Precision

Zero-clearance fitment checks and documented thermal testing.

Performance

Hand-tuned fan curves, EXPO/XMP profiles, and clean power delivery.

Reliability

24-hour stress burn-in and 3-year on-site hardware replacement.

Sustainability

Recyclable foam-molded transit crates and hardware trade-in recycling.
